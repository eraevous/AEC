# Code of conduct

These rules govern participation in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). They
are written in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but
the rules below are the ones that apply.

1. Be respectful and assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
2. No harassment, insults, slurs, or unwelcome personal attention.
3. Disagreement is welcome; this project exists partly to record it. Make your case with evidence,
   and accept the maintainer's decision on scope.

**Reporting.** Open an issue titled "Conduct contact request" with no details, and the maintainer will
arrange a private channel; or contact him through his GitHub profile
([@eraevous](https://github.com/eraevous)). Reports go to the maintainer only and are handled
confidentially.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn or block participants who break
these rules.
