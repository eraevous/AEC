# Code of conduct

AEC follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1. In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome personal attention, in any project space.
- Disagreement is welcome and useful here; this project exists partly to record it.
  Make your case with evidence, and accept the maintainer's decision on scope.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through his GitHub profile
([@eraevous](https://github.com/eraevous)). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
